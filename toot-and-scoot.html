How to play

The goal: blast the toilet paper rolls, grab power-ups, and gas every rival. Clear a level and everyone moves up — new maze, meaner rivals. A fart lingers for a moment, then spreads FAST in four directions. Toot and scoot!

Controller: left stick = move · right stick = camera · B or trigger = fart · A = use power-up · X = superpower · click left stick or LT = sprint · + = pause
Keyboard: WASD + mouse · Space = fart · E = power-up · Q = superpower · Shift = sprint · Esc = pause

Instant boosts (they work the moment you walk over them — already maxed out? the pickup stays on the floor):
Orange = more gas (more farts at once, max 4) · Red = bigger blast (max 5) · Blue = Super Scoot (permanent speed boost, max 7.0). Watch out — rivals grab these too!

Held items (you can hold only ONE — press A to use it; walk over another while your hands are full and it stays on the floor):
Green = trap wall (blocks a tile for 10 seconds — fart + wall = trap!) · White = air freshener (makes any fart near you vanish before it spreads) · Purple = Turbo Toot (aim with your camera and fire — blinds them for 5 seconds!). While holding a wall or Turbo Toot you'll see a glowing aim preview on the floor.

Legendary finds (indestructible — blasts can't destroy them):
The can of beans (10% of drops) — eat it on the spot: for 6 seconds you run at double speed and every tile you cross leaves a fart behind you.
The rainbow Extra Life (2% of drops) — carry it and your avatar shimmers rainbow. When you'd get gassed, it saves you: 2 seconds of invincibility at double speed to escape!

Superpower (X): a different one each level, ONE use per level. Gas Mask = walk through gas for 8 seconds. Silent But Deadly = an invisible, silent fart nobody senses coming. Dutch Oven = trap the nearest rival under a dome with a blast sealed inside.

Sprint and stamina: sprinting gives a speed burst but drains stamina (10 per second of your 100). It refills when you're not sprinting. Run dry and you can't sprint until you catch your breath.

Every 10th level: BOSS FIGHT! The Big Stinker has 1000 health; every blast that hits him does 100 damage. All your abilities work on him. At half health he gets ENRAGED — when glowing warning tiles appear on the floor, scoot off them before they blow!

Scoring: 10 points per roll destroyed · 100 per rival gassed · 200 for gassing another player · 500 for winning a level or round · boss hits 100 each plus a 1000 bonus. Solo high scores go on the leaderboard — enter your initials!

Multiplayer: split screen battle royale with the rivals mixed in. Knocked out? You spectate (A switches who you watch). When someone wins the round, EVERYONE moves on to the next level together, and the win counter keeps the family rivalry honest.
