Toot and Scoot

You're in the maze with your rivals: Sir Toots-a-Lot, Stinky Pete, Windy Wendy, and worse. Out-toot them all!

Move: W A S D  ·  Turn camera: mouse  ·  Drop a fart: Space or click

Controller: left stick = move  ·  right stick = camera  ·  B or trigger = fart  ·  A = use power-up

Pause menu: + button (or Esc on the keyboard)

A fart lingers for a moment, then spreads FAST. Toot and scoot!

Power-ups: orange = more gas · red = bigger blast · blue = Super Scoot · green = trap wall · white = air spray · purple = Turbo Toot

Turbo Toot: face a rival (or another player) and use it — a stink blast blinds them for 5 seconds!

Rare find (10% chance): the can of beans — eat it and for 6 seconds every tile you walk on leaves a fart behind you. Run fast!

Air spray: makes any fart near you disappear before it spreads. Trap wall: blocks a tile for 10 seconds — fart + wall = trap!

Superpower on X (or Q): a different one every level, with a 30 second cooldown between uses! Gas Mask = run through farts for 8 seconds. Silent But Deadly = drop an invisible, silent fart nobody senses coming. Dutch Oven = trap the nearest rival under a dome with a blast inside!

You can only hold ONE power-up — press A (or E on the keyboard) to use it. Grab a second one before using the first and it's wasted!

Clear every rival to move up a level — new maze, more rivals, and they get smarter and faster!
